## 2. Ukraine strikes the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge for a second day; Russia pounds Kyiv's bridges

- Reuters, 17 Nov 2026 (ukraine-strikes-crimean-bridge-second-day-russia-pounds-kyiv-bridges-2026-11-17):
  Ukraine struck the strategic bridge linking southern Russia and Crimea for
  a second straight day on Tuesday, part of what data suggested was the
  largest Ukrainian drone attack of the war; President Zelenskiy confirmed
  the attack and said it retaliated for Russian strikes on Ukrainian
  infrastructure.
- Ukraine's General Staff said an arch and a span of the bridge's road
  section collapsed (corroborated by AP-derived reports, e.g. MTPR/NPR,
  17 Nov 2026). Reuters could not immediately verify social-media footage
  appearing to show the collapsed road arch.
- Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's Russian-installed governor, said two people were
  killed and traffic across the bridge was suspended (attributed claim).
- Russia's Defence Ministry said it shot down 1,441 drones over 22 regions
  (Russian claim). In Kyiv, Mayor Klitschko said Russian forces struck the
  North and South bridges over the Dnipro and partially damaged the metro;
  Moscow confirmed strikes on transport and energy facilities.

**Facts used in the image:** distant bridge with one collapsed span as a

## 5. Humanitarian / health — Congo Ebola outbreak passes 11,000 cases; WHO emergency committee meets

- Reuters, 17 Nov 2026 (Donati): Congo's Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak is
  spreading and killing at a record pace; WHO Director-General Tedros,
  opening the third IHR Emergency Committee meeting on Tuesday, said the
  outbreak now exceeds 11,000 cases and about 5,400 deaths across 67 health
  zones in eight provinces; children account for more than a quarter of
  confirmed cases. ECDC's precise snapshot: 11,376 confirmed cases and
  5,568 deaths as of 15 November. It is the second-largest Ebola outbreak

  the following week (WHO, via AFP-derived reports, 17 Nov 2026).

## 6. Humanitarian — UN: drones account for nearly three-quarters of Sudan's verified civilian deaths

- Reuters, Geneva, 17 Nov 2026: the UN human rights office (OHCHR) said on
  Tuesday it had confirmed 2,230 civilian deaths in Sudan's civil war
  between January and October 2026, of whom over 1,630 — nearly
  three-quarters — were killed by drones; High Commissioner Volker Türk
  condemned the warring parties' "brazen disregard for the laws of war",
  saying violations may amount to war crimes. The UN stressed the verified
  toll is a floor and the true scale is likely higher.
